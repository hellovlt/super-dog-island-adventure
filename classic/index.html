THE SUPER DOG HANDBOOK

Paws, cape, let’s go!

Explore six worlds, rescue five friends, and defeat two bosses. Look for friends, chests, and 18 stars along the way.

  • ← → or A D — run
  • Space / ↑ / W — jump; press again in midair
  • Shift / C — dash through enemies
  • X / F — super bark
  • Esc / P — pause

Use super bark to open chests and cages. Magnets attract bones, shields block a hit, hearts heal you, and lightning boosts your bark. Springs bounce you upward. Flags heal you and save a checkpoint.

On a phone, use the buttons below the game. Progress is saved in this browser: select Continue after reloading. Find the world map beneath the game.
